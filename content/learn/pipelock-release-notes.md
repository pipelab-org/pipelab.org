Most Pipelock releases have a post that explains what changed and why, and many also have an upgrade guide with the configuration and compatibility steps. This page lists every release post on this site, newest first. Some releases, including v2.7, v2.8, and v3.2, have no post here. The [Pipelock changelog on GitHub](https://github.com/pipelab-org/pipelock/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) is the complete record of every version, including patch releases.

## Reading older releases

Older posts describe behavior as it shipped in that version. Later releases changed some defaults: [v3.0](/blog/pipelock-v300-release/) moved enforcement to fail closed, for example. Check the newest upgrade guide before you copy a configuration from an older post.
